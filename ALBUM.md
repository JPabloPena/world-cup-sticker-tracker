2026 Panini FIFA World Cup Sticker Collection: Comprehensive Checklist

1. General Collection Overview and Parallel Variant Guide

The 2026 Panini FIFA World Cup sticker collection represents the most expansive edition in the history of the tournament, accommodating the move to a 48-nation format. The album consists of 112 pages and requires a total of 980 stickers to complete. This total includes exactly 68 special stickers printed on premium foil material. Beyond the standard set, collectors can hunt for 20 "In Action" extra stickers featuring multicolored variants, which are inserted at an average rate of 1 per 100 packets. For digital integration, fans can utilize the FIFA World Cup 2026 Sticker App to scan physical stickers and maintain a digital collection.

North American Border Variants In the North American market, base stickers may feature exclusive border colors. These are classified as follows:

* Blue: Common parallel.
* Red: Exclusive parallel variant.
* Purple: Exclusive parallel variant.
* Green: Exclusive parallel variant.
* Black: 1-of-1 (The rarest variant).
